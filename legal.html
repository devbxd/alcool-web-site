Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to process your order: your name, phone number and delivery address, shared with us directly via WhatsApp when you place an order. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address for that purpose only.

We never sell or share your information with third parties. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to us on WhatsApp.

Terms & Conditions

By using this site and placing an order, you confirm that you are of legal drinking age in Lebanon. Sale of alcohol to minors is strictly prohibited.

Prices are listed in USD and are subject to change without notice. Orders are confirmed manually via WhatsApp once submitted — availability is confirmed at that stage.

Delivery Policy

We deliver across Lebanon. Once your order is confirmed via WhatsApp, delivery typically takes 1–3 business days depending on your location.

Bottles are packaged discreetly and securely. Please ensure someone of legal drinking age is available to receive the delivery.
